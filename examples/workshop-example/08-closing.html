◆ Wrap-up · Three things to keep

Refactor with a net

Takeaway 01
Pin before you touch
Characterization tests record what the code really does — that green baseline is your net.
Takeaway 02
Small green steps
One structural move, verify, commit. If the bar goes red, revert the last step — never debug forward.
Takeaway 03
The gate is human
Nothing ships until a person confirms the behaviour is identical and the shape is genuinely better.
◆ One action
Refactor one module this week
Pick your gnarliest file, pin its behaviour, take a single small green step.
